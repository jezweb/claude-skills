Load the `hermes-tweet` skill.

Parse $ARGUMENTS for:
- **Mode**: `setup`, `brief`, `support`, `launch`, or `action`. Default: `brief`
- **Topic**: query, handle, campaign, tweet, or requested account action

Examples: `/hermes-tweet setup`, `/hermes-tweet brief competitor mentions`, `/hermes-tweet action draft launch reply`
